How to tell

Liberty Head and Indian Head $5 gold pieces from 1839 on are 90% gold.

Melt value vs. selling price

Melt value is the metal alone. What you get for a $5 Half Eagle depends on condition, date and who is buying. Common worn coins usually sell close to melt. Key dates, rare mint marks and high grades can be worth much more, so look those up before selling for metal.

How to tell

Only the 1976-S quarters the Mint sold in its 40% silver three-coin uncirculated and proof sets contain silver. Bicentennial quarters found in circulation are copper-nickel clad, and the S-mint proofs in the regular six-coin proof sets are clad too, so an S mint mark alone does not mean silver. A silver one weighs 5.75 grams and its edge looks silvery gray; a clad one weighs 5.67 grams and shows a copper stripe on the edge.

Melt value vs. selling price

Melt value is the metal alone. What you get for a 1976-S silver Bicentennial quarter depends on condition, date and who is buying. Common worn coins usually sell close to melt. Key dates, rare mint marks and high grades can be worth much more, so look those up before selling for metal.

How to tell

Kennedy halves dated 1965 through 1970 are 40% silver. They look like later clad halves, so go by the date.

Melt value vs. selling price

Melt value is the metal alone. What you get for a 40% Kennedy half depends on condition, date and who is buying. Common worn coins usually sell close to melt. Key dates, rare mint marks and high grades can be worth much more, so look those up before selling for metal.

How to tell

Silver war nickels have a large mint mark (P, D or S) above Monticello on the back. 1942 is split: early 1942 nickels are regular copper-nickel, so check for the big mint mark.

Melt value vs. selling price

Melt value is the metal alone. What you get for a War nickel depends on condition, date and who is buying. Common worn coins usually sell close to melt. Key dates, rare mint marks and high grades can be worth much more, so look those up before selling for metal.

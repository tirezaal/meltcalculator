How to tell

Roosevelt dimes dated 1964 and earlier are 90% silver. 1965 and later circulation dimes are copper-nickel clad with no silver.

Melt value vs. selling price

Melt value is the metal alone. What you get for a Silver Roosevelt dime depends on condition, date and who is buying. Common worn coins usually sell close to melt. Key dates, rare mint marks and high grades can be worth much more, so look those up before selling for metal.

How to tell

Only the S-mint collector Eisenhower dollars (sold in blue packs and brown boxes, plus proofs) are 40% silver. Ikes from circulation are copper-nickel with no silver.

Melt value vs. selling price

Melt value is the metal alone. What you get for a 40% Ike dollar depends on condition, date and who is buying. Common worn coins usually sell close to melt. Key dates, rare mint marks and high grades can be worth much more, so look those up before selling for metal.

How the math works

A new 90% dime holds 0.0723392 troy oz of silver. Quarters and halves are exactly 2.5 and 5 times that, so every dollar of face value in 90% coins holds 0.7234 oz no matter which mix you have. Worn coins weigh a bit less, so most dealers figure 0.715 oz per dollar of face for circulated junk. The calculator uses that by default; switch to full mint weight for uncirculated coins.

40% Kennedy halves hold 0.2958 oz per dollar of face, and 35% war nickels hold 1.1253 oz per dollar of face.

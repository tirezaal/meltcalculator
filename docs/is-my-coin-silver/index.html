Is My Coin Silver?

For US coins the date answers it almost every time. The cutoff is 1964: dimes, quarters and halves dated 1964 or earlier are 90% silver.

By denomination

CoinWhich ones are silver
CentsNone. Pennies were never silver. 1943 steel cents are steel, not silver.
Nickels1942-1945 war nickels with a large mint mark over Monticello are 35% silver. Melt value
Dimes1964 and earlier are 90% silver. 1965 and later circulation dimes are not; silver proof set dimes are the exception. Melt value
Quarters1964 and earlier are 90% silver. 1965 and later circulation quarters are not. Melt value
Half dollars1964 and earlier are 90% silver. 1965-1970 Kennedy halves are 40% silver. 1971 and later circulation halves are not. Melt value
DollarsMorgan and Peace dollars are 90% silver. Some S-mint collector Eisenhower dollars are 40% silver, but S-mint clad proofs also exist, so an S mint mark alone does not mean silver. Susan B. Anthony, Sacagawea and Presidential dollars have no silver. Melt value

Check the edge

Look at the edge of a dime, quarter or half. A 90% silver coin is silver all the way through, so the edge is solid silver colored. A clad coin from 1965 on shows a copper stripe between two layers.

Exceptions to know

Silver proof sets from 1992 on include silver dimes, quarters and halves with later dates. The 1976-S Bicentennial quarter, half and dollar were also sold in 40% silver collector sets. These look like ordinary coins, so check the mint mark and whether they came from a proof or mint set.

Units

Precious metals are weighed in troy ounces. One troy ounce is 31.1034768 grams, a little heavier than a kitchen ounce. A pennyweight (dwt) is 1/20 of a troy ounce and is common for jewelry scrap.

A bar stamped "10 oz .999" holds 9.99 oz of pure silver. Dealers usually price .999 bars and rounds on the stamped weight plus or minus a premium.

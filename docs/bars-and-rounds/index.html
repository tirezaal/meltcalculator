Units

Precious metals are weighed in troy ounces. One troy ounce is 31.1034768 grams, a little heavier than a kitchen ounce. A pennyweight (dwt) is 1/20 of a troy ounce and is common for jewelry scrap.

A piece that weighs 10 troy oz at .999 purity holds 9.99 oz of pure silver. Some makers state the pure content instead, for example "contains 10 oz fine silver." In that case pick Fine metal content and enter 10. A stamp alone doesn't always say which one it means, so check the maker's spec.
